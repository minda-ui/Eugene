# Ask Minda (mechanics only)

Rule source: Hub Coordination Standard, Rule G, and the Task Work Run Standard v1.1 (group Outputs, Drive
`1ysdjb-vsPIQnmGlp2CXIrvyu93LT1ovN`). Owner note for the default rule: group Raw
`2026-10-18_owner-note_work-run-standard-gmail-and-default-rule-approved.md`.

## Ask only for
Money; legal or contract; security; signing; sending anything outward; a choice between ways the business
could go; a fact only she holds. Everything else you decide inside your authority and record.

## Before asking, check (never ask what she already answered)
1. **The Hub:** is there already an open row on this? Add to it rather than opening a second. ⚠️ On the
   700+-row Tasks & Requests sheet, `get_sheet_summary` ignores filters and returns only the oldest ~58
   rows — do not rely on it to decide a row doesn't already exist. Check with `find_in_sheet` (search a
   distinctive word from your question; it pages), or the estate's Open-rows source if one exists.
2. **Her answers:** the owner notes in group Raw (`1AskWaogQoyQH7COKZq85jL00QUtcx---`), newest first.
3. **Drive and your own KB.**

## The row (Tasks & Requests, sheet `8860839228606340`)
Assigned to **Minda**, Requested by your name, Priority High if it blocks money or a date within 7 days
else Medium, Due date = the DEFAULT date. Use the `hub-work-run` skill for the collision-safe Task ID
(find_in_sheet for the max) and the post-add duplicate check. Request in exactly this form:
```
ASK: the question in one line
RECOMMEND: your answer
WHY: one or two lines
NEEDS: yes or no, a number, a file, a name
DEFAULT: what you will do if she has not answered by <date>
```
Related questions share one row (plain ASCII in cells).

## The default
If the DEFAULT date has passed with no answer, you may proceed on your RECOMMEND **only if** the action is
reversible, inside your own authority, and **not** about money, legal, security, signing or sending
outward. Write "proceeded on default <date>" on the row. Otherwise wait and keep the row visible. A draft
is allowed; a send never is.

## After her answer
Write it on the row with the date, act on it, and close with the `hub-work-run` skill. Put any new fact
she gave into your KB so nobody asks twice.
