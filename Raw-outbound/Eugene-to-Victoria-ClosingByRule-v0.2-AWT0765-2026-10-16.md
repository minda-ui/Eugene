## The rule in one screen (v0.2 — this is the block to put into the routine)
CLOSE BY RULE only when it clearly fits ONE of four classes AND no never-close condition applies:
- **Class 1 Marketing** · **Class 2 Auto-acknowledgements** · **Class 3 Automated notices (no action)** ·
  **Class 4 Information-only** (no question to Minda, no deadline, no money).
NEVER close: **money** (incl. finance-service notices e.g. GoCardless) · **real external correspondence** ·
**sharing/permissions/security** · **authority/legal/contract (incl. any solicitor)** · **unknown sender
asking for action** · **anything in doubt**. Seat-owned mail routes to the seat first.
Approved refinements (Minda, 16 Oct):
- **R1** — an internal colleague's OWN outbound/FYI with no question/deadline/money MAY close as Class 4
  even if a recipient is external, UNLESS it touches money/legal/authority/security.
- **R2** — legal/contract/solicitor ALWAYS escalates, whoever sent it.
- **R3** — "unknown sender asking for action" escalates UNLESS a seat has already fully handled it (then
  Class 4).
On close: mark the row resolved with a one-line class reason, append one line to the weekly closed list,
send nothing.
