The loop

  • capture → understand → draft/flag → human decides → record.
  • the record feeds the brain (RAG) + the intelligence.
  • views send people back to act → new capture. It's a cycle.

The gate (multi-SM)

  • Each job has one responsible SM.
  • In-authority → the SM approves; above → Minda.
  • Authority limits = construction-sm-authority-matrix.md.

Intelligence + rhythm

  • Not just a record — predictive flags (slip / overrun / shortfall).
  • Cross-project lessons compound each job.
  • Surfaced on a cadence: morning brief · EOD digest · weekly review.

Views — one source, many cuts

  • SM — their jobs, in the site chat (mobile).
  • Minda — all jobs worst-first + the escalation queue.
  • Client — curated, outward, human-gated.
