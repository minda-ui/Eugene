## English (Eugene's translation)
- The accounting process is a **document-flow schedule** table (who creates, checks, passes on, by when, archives).
- About numbering etc. **Incoming, Outgoing** (registers).
- Sources: USSR Glavarkhiv "Basic rules for departmental archives" (order 05.09.1985 No 263); USSR MinFin
  "Regulation on documents and document flow in accounting" (29.07.1983 No 105).
- Simplified: a document — received, answered. Received — noted — to archive. Case closed — to archive.
- Example reference: **FBC-100/2026-12145** = company / project or department (100) / running number
  (year can be added).
