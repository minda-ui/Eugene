Local or frontier? Checked in this order

1 · PrivacyPrivate data stays local

HR, finance and site photos never go to the cloud, whatever the task.

2 · DifficultyRoutine stays local

The receptionist scores each request. Judgement calls go to an AI employee.

3 · ConfidenceFailed checks go up

Wrong totals, missing sources or failing tests send the work up a level.

4 · StakesIrreversible goes to you

Sending, paying or changing a live system always waits for your approval.

PlannedSecurity of the AI setup

Ten layers: own logins, group-only documents, a sovereign list, Tailscale-only access, no internet for the AI server, every call logged, read-only tools, safe model downloads. Waiting on your approval of the sovereign list.

PlannedMonitoring

A watchtower (Uptime Kuma) with Telegram alerts in three tiers: phones, internet and firewalls first; UPS, servers and backups next; AI last. The watcher is watched from outside, and a daily all-green line means silence is never mistaken for OK.

PlannedSoftware

A frozen, pinned stack for both card types: NVIDIA R580 driver (the last for P40 and V100), CUDA 12, llama.cpp as the engine, Proxmox containers, monthly updates tried at Beverley first. Hardware review around 2028.
