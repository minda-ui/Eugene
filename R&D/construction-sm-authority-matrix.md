## Standing rules (apply to every SM, above the matrix)
- **H&S stop-work is universal** — *any* SM (or anyone) can **stop work for safety at any time**, no limit,
  no escalation needed. Safety never waits.
- **No SM makes a payment** — all payments are Minda / Rachel (the SM *requests*, finance pays).
- **"Not sure" always escalates** — if a decision isn't clearly within the list below, it goes to Minda.
- **Everything is logged** — in-authority or escalated, the decision + who made it is recorded.
- **Limits can be raised per job** — a big/complex job can grant a higher limit for that job (Minda's call).

## Table A — what a site manager may decide on their own job (same for all SMs)
| Decision area | SM may, on their own job… | Escalate to Minda when… |
|---|---|---|
| **Materials / supplies** | order from **approved suppliers** up to their **spend limit** (Table B) | over the limit · a **new supplier/payee** · an unusual/large item |
| **Plant / equipment hire** | hire up to their spend limit | over the limit · a long hire · a new hire company |
| **Subcontractor / labour** | use **approved** subbies / extra hands within the limit | a **new** subbie · over the limit · a new payee |
| **Variation** | agree a variation **within scope** up to their **variation limit** | over the limit · it materially changes price/scope · a client dispute |
| **Programme** | adjust the **daily/weekly** plan + sequencing | change the **completion date** or a **key milestone** |
| **Quality / snags** | manage + close snags; routine QC | **sign off practical completion / handover** |
| **Client communication** | routine site updates + coordination | any **commitment/promise**, pricing, or a **complaint** |
| **H&S** | run the site safely; **stop work anytime** (no limit) | a **RIDDOR/notifiable** event, enforcement, or serious incident → **notify immediately** |
| **Outside-party instructions** | — | instructing a **solicitor / agent / contractor** = **always Minda** (Project-A red flag) |
| **Payments** | — | **all payments = Minda / Rachel** |
| **Anything unusual / "not sure"** | — | **always escalate** |

## How the system uses this
- **Routing:** each job's brief/digest/alerts go to its **responsible SM** (Project Register); Minda gets the
  portfolio + escalations.
- **Approvals:** AI proposes an action → if it's **within that SM's authority** (Table A + their Table B
  limits) it's routed to **that SM** to approve; if **above**, it's routed to **Minda** — one logged path.
- **The brief says who owes a decision:** "Needs you today" lists the in-authority calls for the SM, and
  Minda's brief adds the escalated ones from across all jobs.
