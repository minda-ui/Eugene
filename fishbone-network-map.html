Worth knowing

  • One controller, two sites. The UniFi Cloud Key lives at Beverley and manages the WiFi APs at both sites — if the radio link drops, HQ's APs keep running on cached config but can't be reconfigured until it's back.
  • The phones are centralised. All three desk phones register to the one FusionPBX at HQ (10.224.13.9); Beverley's ext 1006 reaches it across the link (UDP-NAT) — tested with two-way audio 19 Oct.
  • Single points of failure to keep in mind. The airFiber radio is the only native path between sites (the VPN fallback rides the internet). The FusionPBX is one box — now protected by the new off-box backup (pve-nas).
  • Action by 27 Oct — OI-24. Beverley has two BT fibres — the BT Business line is being cancelled (no replacement) and the personal BT line stays, so Beverley keeps internet. The cancelled line also feeds HQ's backup WAN from Beverley over the radio on vlan 4 — re-point anything depending on it before the 27th.
  • Open — OI-20. The UniFi Cloud Key login isn't held yet; WiFi runs fine on cached config, so it's not urgent and never factory-reset it.
  • Minor. The airFiber dashboard clock is wrong (no NTP on the mgmt subnet) and offers a v4.1.0 update — leave firmware alone on a live link.
